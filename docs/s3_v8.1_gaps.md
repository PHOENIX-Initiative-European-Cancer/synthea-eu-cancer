# S3-Leitlinie Prostatakarzinom v8.1 (Aug 2025) — gap analysis vs. the Synthea module

Elements in the S3 pathway that `modules/adult/prostate.json` does **not** yet cover, to guide the next
versions. Every recommendation number/threshold below was verified directly against the official Langversion 8.1 PDF.

**Already covered** (not repeated below): LUTS presentation; DRE/PSA/TRUS; mpMRI+PI-RADS; biopsy; BPH mgmt
(WW/tamsulosin/finasteride/TURP) + incidental cancer at TURP; risk groups (Gleason/ISUP, cTNM); treatment
(AS, RP, EBRT±ADT, RP+PLND); metastatic ADT→ARPI/docetaxel→cabazitaxel→PCa death.

## HIGH priority

### 1. Risk-adapted PSA early-detection (Ch. 4.1, Empf. 4.1–4.10) — biggest v8.1 change
- DRE **no longer** for screening (Empf. 4.1, reversal). PSA offered after counseling to men ≥45 with life
  expectancy ≥10 yr (Empf. 4.2/4.3).
- **Risk-adapted intervals by PSA (Empf. 4.4):** ≤1.5 ng/ml → **every 5 yr**; 1.5–2.99 → **every 2 yr**;
  ≥3 → **work-up (MRI/biopsy)**.
- Family history (≥1 first-degree) → start at 45 (Empf. 4.7). BRCA2/MSH2/MSH6 → risk consult from 40, PSA+MRI
  (Empf. 4.8/4.9), fusion biopsy if PI-RADS 3–5 or PSA ≥3 (Empf. 4.10).
- **Model:** replace/precede the LUTS entry with a screening Guard+Delay loop keyed on a `psa` attribute, three
  interval branches; low-prevalence `brca2_carrier`/`family_history` attributes lower start age + shorten intervals.

### 2. Biochemical recurrence → salvage (Ch. 7.1–7.2) — module jumps cure→metastatic with no recurrence
- BCR definition: post-RP confirmed PSA **>0.2 ng/ml** ×2 (Stmt 7.2); post-RT **nadir + 2 ng/ml** (Phoenix, Stmt 7.3).
- Work-up: PSMA-PET first (Empf. 7.6); PSA-doubling-time + Gleason + latency separate local vs systemic (Empf. 7.7);
  no bone scan if PSA <10 (Empf. 7.8).
- Salvage RT ≥66 Gy, **start early (PSA <0.5)** (Empf. 7.14/7.18); add ADT if PSA >0.7 or unfavorable — **6 mo**
  (raised risk) or **24 mo** (high risk) (Empf. 7.15–7.17). Post-RT recurrence → salvage prostatectomy (7.20) or
  salvage HIFU (7.23). **New 2025:** high-risk BCR → **enzalutamide ± ADT** (EMBARK, Empf. 7.11).
- **Model:** post-curative PSA-surveillance loop → `biochemical_recurrence` (RP vs RT threshold) → PSMA-PET →
  salvage-RT±ADT branch → feeds metastatic if it fails.

### 3. PSMA-PET/CT (Empf. 4.42–4.44 staging; 7.6 recurrence) — imaging currently stops at mpMRI
- Staging only for unfavorable-intermediate / high / very-high risk (Empf. 4.43); PSMA-PET indicated when
  **ISUP ≥3 OR cT3/cT4 OR PSA ≥20** (Empf. 4.44). Primary imaging at recurrence (Empf. 7.6). Positivity scales with
  PSA (33% at <0.2 → 95% at ≥2.0).
- **Model:** conditional imaging encounter gated on the risk-group attribute; reused at the BCR node.

### 4. Risk-group-gated staging investigations (Empf. 4.43; 7.8)
- Low / favorable-intermediate → **no** staging imaging. Conventional bone scan + CT only where PSMA-PET
  unavailable; no bone scan at BCR if PSA <10.
- **Model:** guard on risk group deciding whether staging encounters fire (avoids over-imaging low-risk synthetics).

### 5. Germline/somatic genetic testing → PARP inhibitors (Empf. 4.37–4.38; 7.43–7.44)
- **All** metastatic patients offered human-genetics consult + germline testing (Empf. 4.37); familial syndrome
  likewise (Empf. 4.38). mCRPC + **BRCA1/2**, progressing after ARPI → **olaparib** (Empf. 7.43); chemo not indicated →
  olaparib+abiraterone / niraparib+abiraterone / talazoparib+enzalutamide (Empf. 7.44).
- **Model:** low-prevalence `brca_mutation` attribute at diagnosis; at mCRPC add testing encounter → PARP branch.

## MEDIUM priority

### 6. Lu-177-PSMA and Radium-223 in the mCRPC sequence (Empf. 7.51, 7.52, 7.54–7.55)
- **177Lu-PSMA-617:** post-ARPI, chemo-unfit (Empf. 7.51, new 2025); or post-docetaxel+ARPI alongside cabazitaxel
  (Empf. 7.52). **Radium-223:** symptomatic bone mets, no visceral mets, no other systemic option (Empf. 7.54); must
  **not** combine with abiraterone/prednisone (Empf. 7.55).
- **Model:** extra branches in the enzalutamide→docetaxel→cabazitaxel chain (radium-223 gated on bone-only symptomatic).

### 7. Bone-protective therapy (Empf. 7.72–7.74; rehab Ch. 8.2)
- CRPC + bone mets → **denosumab or zoledronic acid** (Empf. 7.73); **no** bisphosphonates in hormone-naive bone mets
  (Empf. 7.72); dental clearance first (Empf. 7.74). ADT/ARPI bone loss → denosumab/bisphosphonate or **annual DEXA** +
  calcium/vit-D3 (Ch. 8.2).
- **Model:** parallel MedicationOrder alongside ADT/CRPC; optional DEXA loop under long ADT.

### 8. Active-surveillance protocol detail (Empf. 6.6–6.11) — AS state exists, schedule/triggers don't
- Low risk (ISUP 1) → **AS only, local therapy no longer offered** (Empf. 6.6, key v8.1 change). PSA **q6mo (ISUP 1) /
  q3mo (ISUP 2)** first 2 yr (Empf. 6.7). Confirmatory MRI+targeted+systematic biopsy at **12–18 mo** (Empf. 6.8).
  Re-biopsy **≥ every 3 yr within first 10 yr** (Empf. 6.10). Switch triggers (Empf. 6.11): PSA-DT <3 yr → re-MRI/biopsy;
  **stop AS if** upgrade to unfavorable ISUP 2, ≥ISUP 3, or ≥cT3/cN+.
- **Model:** AS monitoring loop with periodic PSA/MRI/biopsy encounters + probabilistic upgrade→treatment.

### 9. Follow-up after curative treatment (Empf. 9.1–9.3)
- First follow-up within **12 weeks**; then **quarterly yr 1–2, half-yearly yr 3–4, yearly from yr 5** (Empf. 9.1).
  PSA is the surveillance test (Empf. 9.2); DRE not routine without BCR (Empf. 9.3). Under ADT: PSA ≥2×/yr.
- **Model:** post-treatment PSA loop with decreasing frequency; its PSA output feeds the BCR detector (item 2).

## LOWER priority

### 10. Focal therapy — HIFU/IRE/cryo/VTP (Empf. 6.77–6.81)
- Only **unilateral low-risk** refusing both standard therapy and AS: Gleason 6, PSA <10, unremarkable DRE, ≤50%
  positive cores one side, mpMRT+fusion diagnosis (Empf. 6.79). Equivalence not proven (Empf. 6.81).
- **Model:** narrow branch off the low-risk node, small population share.

### 11. mHSPC intensification + node-positive treatment (Empf. 7.30–7.31; 6.70)
- New: **darolutamide + ADT**; **triplet ADT + darolutamide + docetaxel** (6 cycles) (Empf. 7.30/7.31). cN+ localized:
  GnRH + **abiraterone/prednisone for 2 yr** (Empf. 6.70).
- **Model:** ARPI+ADT and triplet as alternative metastatic entries; cN+ branch off localized high-risk.

### 12. Care-plan adornments
Nerve-sparing at RP; continence + erectile rehab incl. pelvic-floor training (Ch. 8.1); psycho-oncology;
palliative care split into Supportivtherapie (7.8) vs non-curative (7.9); interdisciplinary tumor board.
Best modeled as CarePlan/Encounter/Procedure adornments, not new decision logic.

## ESMO cross-check (2026-07-02)

Held the European **ESMO** guideline (2020 Parker CPG + 2023 update + 2025 localized CPG + 2026 advanced/metastatic
living CPG) against the S3/EAU calibration. **Result: the current ESMO has largely converged with our calibration** —
apparent differences are mostly 2020-vs-current ESMO artifacts, not ESMO-vs-EAU disagreements.

- **AGREES (no change):** risk stratification (D'Amico/EAU tiers, favourable/unfavourable split), risk-adapted
  screening philosophy, mpMRI-first diagnostics, localized ADT durations (4–6 mo intermediate / 2–3 yr high),
  early salvage RT (<0.5 ng/ml), survival anchors.
- **Genuine divergences (model keeps S3):** screening entry age S3 45/40 vs ESMO >50/>45/>40 (keep S3); ADT-duration
  wording 18–36 mo vs 2–3 yr (cosmetic).
- **Do NOT implement:** ESMO-2020-only neoadjuvant docetaxel before RT for very-high-risk localized (dropped later; not S3/EAU).
- **ESMO-ADDS to adopt:** move germline/HRR testing upstream to **all mCSPC** (not family-history- or mCRPC-gated);
  somatic BRCA panel for all mCRPC; encode mCRPC **PSMA-PET gating for Lu-177** (≥1 PSMA-positive / no PSMA-negative;
  7.4 GBq q6w ×6), **first-line ARPI+PARP** for HRR+, and **no ARPI→ARPI** switch (CARD). → folded into beads tn0.

*ESMO sources: Ann Oncol 2020;31(9):1119-1134; 2023 update (Fizazi & Gillessen); 2025 local/locoregional CPG; 2026 advanced/metastatic living CPG.*
